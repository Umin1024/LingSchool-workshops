黄越敏是一位多媒体艺术家与艺术教育者。她本科毕业于华东师范大学公共艺术专业，后在芝加哥艺术学院（SAIC）获得艺术与技术研究方向艺术硕士学位，目前任职于华东师范大学美术学院，担任讲师。她是艺术游戏团体 [[rect*]]repair 的成员，该团体从事实验性游戏创作、社区活动与公共介入。她的个人创作关注媒介的形式条件与基础设施的物质性——探索它如何塑造并规训社会生活。她以游戏和互动媒体为方法，持续追问感知与行动之间的裂缝。

Yuemin Huang is a multimedia artist and art educator. She holds a BA in Public Art from East China Normal University and an MFA in Art and Technology Studies from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, and currently teaches as a lecturer in the Department of Art and Design at East China Normal University. She is a member of the art-game collective [[rect*]]repair, which makes and facilitates experimental games, community events, and public interventions. Her own practice concerns the formal conditions of media and the materiality of infrastructure — how it shapes and conditions social life. Working through games and interactive media, she keeps probing the gap between how we perceive and how we act.
